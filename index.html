NORSOK M-506 Rev. 2
CO2 Corrosion Calculator

The NORSOK corrosion model estimates CO2 corrosion in carbon steel systems using process conditions such as temperature, pressure, pH, flow regime, wall shear stress, and fluid composition. It supports engineering assessment of pipelines and production facilities by applying recognized industry guidance to predict corrosion rate trends and compare operating scenarios for safer material selection, monitoring, mitigation, and maintenance planning decisions.

de Waard-Milliams
CO2 Corrosion Model

The de Waard-Milliams model predicts CO2 corrosion in carbon steel by relating corrosion rate to operating temperature, CO2 partial pressure, fluid chemistry, and environmental correction factors. It is widely used for oil and gas production systems to estimate baseline sweet corrosion risk, screen operating conditions, and support decisions on inhibition, inspection frequency, and pipeline integrity management.
